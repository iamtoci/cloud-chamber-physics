What does the trail tell us?

  1. 01

    Charged radiation can ionise atoms or molecules.

  2. 02

    The chamber makes effects of that ionisation visible.

  3. 03

    A dense, short trail is evidence of strong ionisation, not certain identification.

!

One important distinction: gamma rays have no charge, so they do not make a direct condensation trail. They can sometimes produce a visible secondary electron.
