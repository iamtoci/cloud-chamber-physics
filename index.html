✳ THE KEY IDEA IN THREE STEPS

Why does a trail appear?

  1. 01

    A charged particle passes through the vapour.

  2. 02

    It ionises molecules along its path.

  3. 03

    Vapour condenses on the ions as tiny droplets: a visible trail.

!

One important distinction: gamma rays have no charge, so they do not make a direct condensation trail. They can sometimes produce a visible secondary electron.
